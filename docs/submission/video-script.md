## Going offline in DevTools (exact steps)

1. In Chrome, press **F12** (or right-click the page, then **Inspect**).
2. Click the **Network** tab.
3. Find the throttling dropdown that says **No throttling** and choose **Offline**. A warning icon appears on the tab.
4. Click **Check my report** and wait for the result and the **Checked on your device** badge.
5. Set the dropdown back to **No throttling** before the Outbox step, otherwise the cloud step fails.

## OBS shot list checklist

- [ ] Chrome only, window capture, 1920x1080, 30 fps, no bookmarks bar, no other tabs, notifications off.
- [ ] Browser zoom 110 percent so the text is readable.
- [ ] Gemini Nano is ready (`await LanguageModel.availability()` returns "available"); do a full dry run first.
- [ ] Scene 1: title card and the invented "night-shift nurse" slide.
