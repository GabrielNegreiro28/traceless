## Going offline in DevTools (exact steps)

1. In Chrome, press **F12** (or right-click the page, then **Inspect**).
2. Click the **Network** tab.
3. Find the throttling dropdown that says **No throttling** and choose **Offline**. A warning icon appears on the tab.
4. Before filming, do one online scan in the same tab first (this loads the text analyser), and keep **Disable cache** in the Network tab unchecked.
5. Click **Check my report** and wait for the result and the **Checked on your device** badge.
6. Set the dropdown back to **No throttling** right after the badge, before **Continue**; otherwise the cloud step fails.
7. Plan several takes: an on-device scan can stall, and a retake is normal.
